Engineering priorities

What we are looking for

Biometric sensing

Heart rate with documented sampling behavior, SpO₂, HRV, sleep and steps; temperature only with reliable evidence.

Local-first connectivity

Documented BLE SDK/protocol, native iOS/Android examples, local historical data access and no mandatory manufacturer cloud.

Deployment readiness

Good battery life, OTA/firmware roadmap, configurable alerts, OEM branding, test reports and traceable hardware revisions.

Unsupported non-invasive blood glucose or blood pressure claims will not be accepted as validated measurements. ECG and clinical features require separate evidence and regulatory assessment. All patient-data processing must meet applicable privacy and security requirements, including Brazil's LGPD.

Supplier evaluation

How to participate

We welcome qualified manufacturers to share a technical and commercial evaluation package:

  • One to three complimentary engineering samples, preferably with sponsored shipping; no shipment without prior coordination.
  • Free evaluation access to BLE SDK/protocol documentation and working Android/iOS examples.
  • Clear explanation of local historical-data access, offline behavior, timestamps, OTA and cloud dependencies.
  • EXW quotations for 100, 500, 1,000 and 3,000 units; OEM MOQ, branding, lead times and warranty.
  • Hardware revision, component specifications, biometric accuracy evidence and available RF/test reports.
  • Cooperation with the Brazilian applicant, designated certification body and laboratory as required.
